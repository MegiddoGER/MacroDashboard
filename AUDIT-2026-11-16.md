# MacroDashboard — Audit of analysis logic, weighting and the learning loop

_2026-11-16 · HEAD `15f9683` · 749 tests pass · read-only audit, no code or data changed_

Every finding below was reproduced, not inferred: against the live database
(read-only), against Yahoo data fetched today (yfinance 1.2.0), or by running
the engines with constructed inputs. No outcome statistics were computed on data
after the holdout boundary; the holdout counter still reads 0.

Severity: **S1** = produces a wrong number or statement the user acts on, or
silently breaks the measurement. **S2** = logic or weighting defect. **S3** = minor.

---

## 0. Verdict

1. **The learning loop is broken in two places since October.** Live outcomes
   are abandoned before they can be evaluated (0.9 % of October's 7-day results
   exist), and the weekly catch-up job re-records the entire nine-year history
   on a shifted grid each run (188k → 768k snapshots, 98 % of the additions are
   duplicates in time). Both are recoverable.
2. **The fundamental block feeds on corrupted inputs.** The DCF values Microsoft
   at −0.94 USD and TSMC at +1,776 % upside; "insider buying" is stock grants;
   a 222 % payout ratio scores as "sustainable"; the dividend yield in the page
   header is 100× too high for every stock.
3. **The weights do not do what they say.** Measured on 6,905 live snapshots,
   trend + volume (30 % nominal weight) produce 73 % of the score's movement;
   oscillator + fundamental (65 % nominal) produce 25 %.
4. **No reweighting of the present inputs can add return** — that is the
   project's own measured result (CONTEXT.md §2b, §2j) and this audit found
   nothing that contradicts it. The one validated input, net issuance (§2p–§2s),
   is still not in the score and its data has not been refreshed since 2026-09-09.
5. The position engine has four reproducible logic bugs, one of which shows
   "hold, no clear recommendation" for a position with every warning active.

## 1. Learning machine (owner's priority 1)

### L1 · S1 — Outcomes are abandoned before the price exists

`outcomes_nachtragen` (`snapshot_engine/snapshot_service.py:817-889`) retries a
due outcome at most `MAX_OUTCOME_VERSUCHE = 5` times. The drain job runs every
3 minutes (`scheduler.py:43`). `kurs_am_stichtag` returns `None` whenever the due
timestamp lies after the last available bar (`services/market_data_batch.py:168-170`)
— which is always the case on the due day itself, because the due timestamp
carries a time of day and daily bars are stamped 00:00. Five attempts are spent
within 15 minutes; the outcome then drops out of the query for good.

It only "worked" while the app was not running continuously. Since autostart
(2026-09-09) it runs all day:

| Live 7-day outcomes, by due month | evaluated | abandoned | share evaluated |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08 | 501 | 4 | 99.2 % |
| 2026-09 | 736 | 860 | 46.1 % |
| 2026-10 | 35 | 3,968 | **0.9 %** |
| 2026-11 | 327 | 877 | 27.2 % |

Stuck at exactly `versuche = 5`, due more than three days ago: LIVE 5,711 /
4,085 / 226 (7 / 30 / 90 days), HISTORISCH 704 / 5,160 / 4,959.

**Fix.** Count an attempt only when the downloaded series already extends past
the due date (or do not select outcomes before `faellig_am + 2 days`). Then reset
once: `versuche = 0 WHERE ausgewertet = 0`. **Nothing is lost** — the prices are
still available, the rows are merely parked.

### L2 · S1 — The catch-up job re-grids the whole history every week

`aufholung_pruefen` (`backfill_service.py:179-232`, added 2026-10-04) starts a new
full replay every 7 days. The replay grid begins at bar 250 of whatever the
download returns (`:400`), and the download start moves with the calendar (`:279`).
So each run lands on a different 7-bar phase, and `_ticker_replayen` only skips
exact date matches (`:390-407`). The docstring assumes existing dates would be
skipped; they are not.

| Job | started | snapshots added | first AAPL date |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3 (the clean re-recording) | 09-03 | 188,347 | 2017-08-04 |
| 4 | 10-04 | 44,263 | 2017-09-01 |
| 5 | 10-12 | 130,000 | 2017-09-12 |
| 6 | 10-20 | 147,801 | — |
| 7 | 10-29 | 180,623 | 2017-09-29 |
| 8 | 11-09 | 28,573 | — |
| 9 (running now) | 11-16 | 48,370 so far | 2017-10-17 |

Of the 579,630 rows added by jobs 4–9, **568,676 (98.1 %) lie inside the period
job 3 already covered.** Half of all consecutive historical snapshots of a ticker
are now one day apart. AAPL has 16 snapshots in October 2025 instead of three.

Consequences:

- `basis.KADENZ_HANDELSTAGE = 7` (`auswertung/basis.py:36`) is no longer true.
  Every **absolute** statistic (hit rates, calibration, indicator leaderboards,
  the start-page card, Kelly) now overstates its effective sample by 4.08× —
  real error margins are about **twice** as wide as displayed. At saturation
  (all seven phases) it will be 7×.
- The new rows carry **no benchmark return** (3,611 of 1.71 million outcomes
  do). Market-relative statistics therefore still rest on job 3 alone, which
  keeps them honest, but their coverage has dropped to about a quarter.
- "Es gibt nur noch EINE Generation" (CONTEXT.md §1) no longer holds, and no
  evaluation filters on `backfill_job_id`.
- Each run replays nine years × 592 tickers (job 7 ran three days). Database:
  5.5 GB, 7.8 million indicator rows.

**Fix.** Continue each ticker's grid from its last existing HISTORISCH date
(+7 bars) instead of from bar 250. **Immediate stop:** cancel job 9 in the UI —
by design the catch-up does not renew after an abort. **Cleanup is the owner's
decision** (destructive): delete the rows of jobs 4–9 dated up to 2026-09-03,
after a backup. Findings in CONTEXT.md §2–§2s are unaffected; they were measured
before the first catch-up or on the price-panel path.

### L3 · S1 — The live cadence compares two different scores

The gating step derives a direction from the **technical-only** score and asks
whether it differs from the last stored live snapshot, whose direction comes
from the **full** score (`snapshot_service.py:584-585` against `:502-518`). The
two disagree on 40.1 % of live rows, so "direction changed" fires without any
change. Of 7,307 consecutive live pairs of the same ticker, 4,089 (56 %) are less
than seven days apart — 2,378 of them with identical direction, 779 less than
a day apart. The live series is oversampled exactly where technical and fundamental
views conflict.

**Fix.** Compare like with like: keep the gating direction on the snapshot and
compare against the previous gating direction.

### L4 · S1 — Fundamentals are recorded as ±1 only, on the series that cannot be replayed

`indikatoren_schreiben` takes the fundamental indicators from the display
checklist and drops every neutral row (`snapshot_service.py:358-376`). In the live
record, **0 of 22,498 fundamental rows carry a raw value**; the DCF appears on
4,774 of 6,905 snapshots and nothing distinguishes "neutral" from "not
available" on the rest. This is defect BC-04 (CONTEXT.md §2h/§2j) again — on the
half of the score that, unlike the technical half, can never be re-recorded.

**Fix.** Let `_score_fundamental` write the measured quantities into `signals`
(DCF upside, balance score, insider counts, institutional share, analyst mean,
payout, streak) and list them in `_SIGNAL_INDIKATOREN` with a raw-value key.
The past is partly recoverable by parsing `checklist_json`.

### L5 · S2 — The live series has no training segment

By date, every live snapshot lies after the holdout boundary. The first look at
live outcomes for the fundamental block is therefore formally a holdout read.
It needs its own boundary before anyone looks.

## 2. Analysis correctness — wrong numbers on the page

### A1 · S1 — The DCF runs on a Yahoo field that is not free cash flow

`calc_dcf_valuation` reads `info["freeCashflow"]` (`services/fundamental.py:53`).
Today, against the free cash flow in the same companies' cash-flow statements:

| | `info.freeCashflow` | statement FCF | DCF result |
|---|---|---|---|
| MSFT | 3.0 bn | 67.0 bn | fair value **−0.94 USD**, "−100.2 %", vote −1 |
| NVDA | 41.8 bn | 96.7 bn | fair value 23.50 against 155, "−84.9 %", vote −1 |
| GOOGL | −1.2 bn | 73.3 bn | falls into the "FCF negative, 10 % of revenue" proxy |
| O (REIT) | 1.6 bn | 4.0 bn | "−86.5 %", vote −1 |

In the live record the DCF votes −1 on 3,209 snapshots and +1 on 1,565: it calls
two thirds of the universe mispriced by more than 20 %, mostly downward.

**Fix.** Take operating cash flow minus capex from the statements (the SEC
point-in-time data is already in `accrual_kennzahlen` for US filers), average
over 2–3 years, and exclude financials and REITs from the DCF outright.

### A2 · S1 — Share classes

`sharesOutstanding` (`fundamental.py:54`) counts one share class. Alphabet: 5.89 bn
against 12.29 bn `impliedSharesOutstanding` — per-share value overstated 2.09×.
Applies to GOOGL, GOOG and ABEA.DE. **Fix:** prefer `impliedSharesOutstanding`.

### A3 · S1 — Currencies inside the DCF

Price is in the trading currency, cash flow, debt and cash in `financialCurrency`.
TSMC (TWD statements, USD price): fair value **6,997.72 against 373, "+1,776 %"**,
vote +1, and the text "klare fundamentale Unterbewertung". SAP (NYSE) and every
Xetra listing of a US company (ABEA.DE, MSF.DE …) are off by the EUR/USD rate.
The risk-free rate is the US 10-year for all companies. **Fix:** convert with the
existing FX service when the two currencies differ; skip the DCF if no rate.

The position score additionally uses the proxy DCF that the entry score
deliberately excludes (`position_scoring.py:237-250` has no `is_proxy_fcf` check).

### A4 · S1 — "Insider buying" is stock grants

Yahoo's `insider_purchases` counts awards and exercises as purchases:

| | Yahoo, last 6 months | open-market buys in own SEC table since 2024 |
|---|---|---|
| MSFT | 17 purchases, 518,348 shares | 2 (8,842 shares) |
| AAPL | 11 purchases, 838,588 shares | 0 |
| NVDA | 18 purchases, 2.6 m shares | 0 |

The rule at `scoring.py:685` turned this into +1 and the sentence "Insider kaufen
aktiv eigene Aktien — ein starkes Vertrauenssignal" on **2,706 of 6,905 live
snapshots (39 %)**. The correct data sits unused in `insider_geschaefte`
(806,862 Form-4 rows). And by the project's own measurement (§2o) cluster buying
is not a signal. **Fix:** read the SEC table, show it as information, score 0.

### A5 · S1 — Dividend: three independent errors

- **Yield unit.** yfinance now returns percent (AAPL `0.3`, KO `2.14`).
  `templates/partials/analysis_content.html:32` multiplies by 100 — the header
  shows **30.00 % for Apple, 214 % for Coca-Cola**, for every stock. The `< 1`
  heuristic in `fundamental.py:369/396` shows Apple at 30.0 %; the peer table
  (`:339`) multiplies again; `valuation.py:111,144` compare against 0.04 / 0.05.
- **Payout ≥ 100 %.** `payoutRatio` is a fraction; the `< 1` heuristic
  (`fundamental.py:370/397`) leaves 2.22 (Realty Income, 222 %) as "2.2 %", which
  passes `0 < payout < 60` at `scoring.py:832` → **"Nachhaltig", +1**. The most
  unsustainable payouts get the best mark.
- **Streak.** Annual sums by calendar year compare the running year against a
  full one: KO and JNJ show streak 0 in November, so the +1 is reachable only
  late in the year. Years without dividends are skipped, so AAPL shows 24
  (1995 and 2012 count as adjacent). `growth_rate` runs from a partial first to a
  partial last year.

### A6 · S1 — Mixed currencies in the position path and position sizing

`stats["current_price"]` is converted to EUR (`services/market_data.py:248`); the
price history stays in the original currency. The position path combines them
(`routers/analysis.py:943, 979-986`), as does position sizing (`:736-741`).
MSFT today: "current price" 470.51 (EUR) against a last close of 517.78 (USD).

| | computed | same currency |
|---|---|---|
| Distance to SMA 20 | −11.0 % | −2.0 % |
| Drawdown from the 1-year high | −15.9 % | −7.4 % |
| Chandelier stop | 514.40, above the "price" → silently dropped | valid |

Profit giveback, MAE/MFE and every ATR stop inherit the error. EUR listings are
unaffected, which is why it has not shown on the owner's own positions.
**Fix:** one currency per calculation — pass the unconverted close into the
engines and convert only for display.

### A7 · S1 — The score on the page is not the score being measured

The analysis page scores a 1-year window (`routers/analysis.py:177`), the live
snapshots a 2-year window (`snapshot_service.py:645`), the backfill 500 bars. The
FVG count is "unmitigated gaps since the window began" and grows with the window
(AMD +11 against +26). Today, on 20 tickers: 4 different confidences, 3 different
labels — NVDA 44.9 / 54.1, TSLA 36.2 (Verkauf) / 45.4 (neutral), INTC 51.0
(neutral) / 60.3 (Kauf). **Fix:** score the same 2-year window everywhere, and
bound the FVG lookback so it stops depending on window length.

### A8 · S2 — Sentiment is saturated and a third of the lexicon is dead

- VADER looks up single tokens; the **36 multi-word entries** never match:
  "raises guidance" 0.00, "chapter 11" 0.00, "price target raised" 0.00,
  "record low" +0.03, "profit warning" **+0.13**.
- 80.6 % of live readings are ≥ 0.15 (median 0.41): "Stark Bullish, +2 of 2" is
  the default state, not a signal — the same pattern §2h describes for a flag set
  on 62 % of observations.
- Recency weighting is by list position (`sentiment.py:231`), not by time; the
  first headline carries about 20 %.
- The euphoria trap (`scoring.py:992`, −15 confidence points, never measured)
  therefore reduces to "RSI > 70 while news exists".

**Fix:** Loughran–McDonald word lists or FinBERT instead of extending VADER;
score against the ticker's own rolling median; weight by age in days. Until
then, display only.

### A9 · S2 — Action texts assert what the measurement refutes

`calc_technical_summary` still prints "Starkes Setup. Entry bei Pullback …",
"Attraktives Trendfolge-Setup", "Falling Knife" from the confidence bands
(`services/technical.py:738-753`, shown at `analysis_content.html:1112`). §2m
renamed the number to "Einigkeit" because it does not separate against the
market; the sentence beneath it still reads as a trade instruction.

## 3. Weighting (owner's priority 2)

### W1 — Nominal weight against measured influence

6,905 live full-score snapshots, score version 2.2.0. "Influence" is each
category's share of the variance of the weighted score.

| Category | nominal weight | **measured influence** | mean | non-zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| trend | 0.18 | **52.4 %** | 0.00 | 98.7 % |
| volume | 0.12 | **20.7 %** | +0.01 | 100 % |
| oscillator | 0.35 | **12.9 %** | −0.01 | 50.5 % |
| fundamental | 0.30 | **12.5 %** | +0.16 | 93.0 % |
| sentiment | 0.05 | **1.5 %** | +0.19 | 25.9 % |

Correlations: trend~volume +0.57, volume~oscillator −0.58, trend~oscillator −0.28.

Why: trend and volume are votes that never abstain (±1 on every snapshot) and
reinforce each other; the oscillator abstains half the time and, when it speaks,
speaks against them. The category with the largest weight mostly cancels. This
is §2 ("Gewicht ist nicht Einfluss") measured for the first time on the full
score, including fundamentals.

### W2 — The stated reasons for the current weights are refuted

The comment block at `scoring.py:153-203` justifies oscillator 0.35 (0.65 in the
screener) as "einzige Kategorie mit belegtem Vorsprung" and "durch den Replay
unabhängig bestätigt". That finding was absolute, on binary coding, and was
withdrawn in §2b and §2j. The weights were never revisited.

### W3 — Structural defects in the sum

- **Half-point items dilute.** Congress and institutional share add 1 to
  `cat_max` but at most 0.5 to the score (`scoring.py:746-748, 788-790`).
- **A one-sided constant.** Institutional share can only add; it fired +0.5 on
  78 % of live snapshots. It also separates US from German listings rather than
  good from bad.
- **Optimism constants.** Analyst consensus: +1 on 2,719, −1 on 216. Earnings
  surprise by sign: 1,480 positive, 259 negative. Fundamental mean +0.16.
- **Missing categories compress toward 50.** `_finalize_score` leaves the weight
  of an empty category in the denominator (`scoring.py:971-981`), although
  `_renormalisierte_gewichte` exists for exactly this and documents why
  (`:1189-1201`). A ticker without fundamentals and news cannot exceed 82.5,
  while the label thresholds (60 / 75) stay fixed.
- **Three votes, one measurement.** RSI, Stochastic and %B are one quantity;
  FVG and SMA-200 distance correlate at 0.73 (§2j); VWMA, OBV slope and POC are
  momentum (BC-01).

### W4 — Recommendation

Changing the five numbers will not help; §2b/§2j measured that, and the
forecast-combination literature says the same from the other side — estimated
weights rarely beat equal weights unless the evidence for a difference is
strong. What is worth doing, in this order:

1. **Make weight equal influence.** Score each block from its continuous raw
   value (already recorded since §2j), expressed as a percentile against the
   ticker's own two-year history, mapped to [−1, +1]. Then 0.30 means 0.30.
2. **One vote per independent quantity.** Merge trend + volume into one
   trend/momentum block; average the three oscillators into one stretch block.
3. **Two axes instead of one sum.** *Substance* (slow: net issuance, balance
   sheet, a repaired valuation) and *timing* (fast: trend, stretch). They live on
   different horizons (§4 P1-06, P2-05), and trend and mean reversion are
   opposing hypotheses about the same price path — netting them is what
   produces the cancellation in W1.
4. **Weight by evidence.** Equal weights across blocks until live data says
   otherwise. One exception is supported: **net issuance as a size-graded
   avoidance penalty** — full in liquidity deciles 2–5, tapering upward, bottom
   decile excluded (§2s). This is the pending decision in CONTEXT.md §5.
5. **Renormalise missing categories**, as the technical score already does.
6. **Info only, score 0:** institutional share, Congress, analyst level, insider
   activity, sentiment (until A8), the euphoria trap.

Items 1, 2, 5 and 6 change `cat_scores` → `SCORE_VERSION` 3.0.0 under the rule
in CONTEXT.md §7.

### W5 · S2 — Kelly mixes two definitions and reports market drift

`kelly_parameter` (`auswertung/risk_adjusted.py:86-92`) does not pass directions:
the win rate is direction-aware over KAUF **and** VERKAUF, the win/loss ratio is
direction-blind — the case the docstring of `kennzahlen_aus_returns` warns about.
It pools live and historical rows including the re-grid (n = 455,645,
"n_eff" 148,844). Current output: W 51.1 %, R 1.137 → 4.0 % half-Kelly, the same
for every stock.

On the clean grid (job 3, training part, 30 days): after a KAUF signal the
stock was up 56.1 % of the time at R 1.04 — after a **VERKAUF** signal it was up
about 60 % of the time. The Kelly figure is the equity premium, not the score. It also describes a 30-day hold, while the
position next to it is a 1.5 / 2.5 ATR bracket. **Fix:** drop the Kelly tile
until the journal holds 30+ closed trades and compute it from their R-multiples;
keep the fixed-risk sizing, which is sound.

### W6 · S2 — The start-page hit rate has no reference point

`cached_hit_rate(90)` pools live and historical, buy and sell, absolute, with the
inflated n_eff. `basis.py` itself says a hit rate without its base rate is
meaningless. Show the lead over the base rate and against the market, or nothing.

## 4. Position engine

### P1 · S1 — More warnings, milder advice

The state engine sets `EXIT_REVIEW` at five or more technical warnings
(`position_state_engine.py:139-141`); `generate_recommendation` has no branch for
it (`recommendation_engine.py:139-210`), and `THESIS_REVIEW` is never set.

| Position | warnings | state | recommendation shown |
|---|---|---|---|
| +8 % | 4 | PROFIT_PROTECTION / ELEVATED | "Gewinnsicherung prüfen" |
| +8 % | **9 (all)** | EXIT_REVIEW / HIGH | **"Trailing Stop empfohlen"** — milder |
| −10 % | **9 (all)** | EXIT_REVIEW / HIGH | **"Keine klare Handlungsempfehlung. Position beobachten."** |

The warnings are also one measurement counted six times (price below SMA 20,
below SMA 50, below VWMA, MACD, OBV slope); an ordinary pullback below the
SMA 50 in an intact uptrend reaches six points.

### P2 · S1 — Suggested take-profit below the current price

`calculate_suggested_take_profit` anchors at the entry (`trailing_stop_engine.py:313-319`).
Entry 100, price 112, suggested stop 97 → **target 106**. The engine lists exactly
this under forbidden actions. **Fix:** anchor at the current price.

### P3 · S1 — MACD and OBV relevance are always bullish

`"" in entry.get("Signal", "")` is always true (`position_analysis.py:83, 108, 110`;
an emoji was removed from the literal). Bearish MACD → "Momentum unterstützt die
Position — Gewinne laufen lassen." OBV distribution → "Institutionelle Käufe
stützen die Position."

### P4 · S1 — Changing the size modifier silently changes the analysis

The re-render imports `calc_dcf_valuation` from `services.valuation`
(`routers/analysis.py:1213`); it lives in `services.fundamental`. The `ImportError`
is swallowed, and balance data, initial stop and previous stop are not passed
either. Valuation, balance score and R-multiple vanish, and the overall score
moves for reasons unrelated to position size.

### P5 · S2 — Smaller points

- Three of the twelve sub-scores are always `None` (relative strength, quality,
  event risk; `position_scoring.py:234, 274, 339`). Earnings dates and benchmark
  series are both in the project.
- `signals["dcf_upside"]` is never set outside a test; the "DCF zeigt starkes
  Bewertungsrisiko" warning cannot fire (`recommendation_engine.py:213`).
- "Kurs unter SMA20 trotz intaktem übergeordnetem Trend" is appended without
  checking the trend (`:217-220`).
- "Gross" positions get the tightest proposal, with no minimum distance; it can
  be an SMA 20 a fraction of a percent away. Size, not stop width, is the lever.
- The SHORT path is not mirrored in the state engine, the sub-scores or the
  Chandelier stop. Correctly not exposed in the UI; keep it that way.
- `position_scoring.py:215-228` still reads "not computable" as bearish — the
  §2j defect, fixed only in the recording path.

  close written as the snapshot price (`snapshot_service.py:658`).

## 6. Goal check

Stated goal: measure whether the recommendations come true and improve the
analysis from that automatically; ultimately, earn money with it.

- **The measuring discipline is the project's real asset** — market-relative
  baselines, effective sample size, Šidák, year stability, an untouched holdout.
  It is stricter than the literature it draws on.
- **What it has found:** ten technical families without a market-relative lead;
  one validated input (net issuance), strongest as an avoidance filter; one weak
  candidate (earnings misses, 7 days).
- **"Automatic improvement" does not exist yet,** and should not until there is
  something to improve toward: no result feeds back into a weight. Before that
  can be built, L1–L4 must hold, and A1–A5 must be fixed — otherwise the live
  loop will faithfully measure a DCF that values Microsoft below zero.
- **Where the tool can earn its keep today** is avoidance and risk: the net
  issuance filter, sizing by fixed risk, stops. That part has bugs A6 and P1–P4
  in it.
- No open positions are recorded (`positions` is empty), so the position path
  and the journal are not collecting.
